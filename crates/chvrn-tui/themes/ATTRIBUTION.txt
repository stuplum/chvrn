Bundled Helix themes
===================

All ten .toml files in this directory are unmodified copies from one Helix
revision, including their original author and provenance comments:

Repository: https://github.com/helix-editor/helix
Commit: ba40e547426b0f9896c8bdc699a4ab11f2b37dbc
Source directory:
https://github.com/helix-editor/helix/tree/ba40e547426b0f9896c8bdc699a4ab11f2b37dbc/runtime/themes

Files: darcula.toml, catppuccin_latte.toml, catppuccin_frappe.toml,
catppuccin_macchiato.toml, catppuccin_mocha.toml, gruvbox.toml,
tokyonight.toml, github_light.toml, solarized_dark.toml, solarized_light.toml.

Inheritance is fully contained here: catppuccin_latte, catppuccin_frappe and
catppuccin_macchiato inherit catppuccin_mocha. The other files have no parent.
No network access or installed Helix is required at runtime.
